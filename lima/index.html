What do the two packages cost?

US$1,000 per player for a two-person apartment, or US$1,499 per player for Voss House with three players. Both cover your own bedroom in Miraflores for 14 nights, 7–21 October. Choose the atmosphere and apartment that fit you.

What cash games can I play at Atlantic City?

Atlantic City has live cash throughout the week. The usual game is S/5–S/10 with a S/500 minimum buy-in; S/10–S/25 can run when demand is there. It is on Av. Benavides 430 in Miraflores, a few blocks from our planned base. We check the lineup that day; the casino manages its own tables.

When does the Voss Room play?

Tuesday and Thursday nights, starting Tuesday 13 October. During your stay that means 13, 15 and 20 October, at S/10–S/25. It is our separate private game in Miraflores; message me to join the lineup.

I'm flying in. How do I see the real apartment?

You don't need to be in Lima. I'll send current photos and a video walkthrough of the exact apartment and your bedroom, with the location and layout. If you want, we can do a video call before you decide. The photos on this page show the atmosphere, not the actual rental.

What's included, and what do I pay separately?

Your 14-night stay and private bedroom are the core package. I'll coordinate the group, help with arrival and organize two social nights each week. Your bankroll, casino or Voss Room buy-ins, flights, food, drinks and club expenses are yours. I'll confirm the final apartment and arrival transfer with your offer.

How do I reserve from another country?

Message me on WhatsApp with DUO or VOSS HOUSE. I'll send the available apartment, real photos and video, the exact price for your room, payment options and reservation terms. Choose your stay, then pay through the method we agree on; I'll confirm your room directly.
